### 1. Revenue Analytics Power BI
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a61cea35-3ef0-4da7-9838-98bdbecf3313)
Built a dashboard that streamlines revenue analytics reporting to support P&L reviews and Corporate Consolidation requirements. It introduces a volume-weighted AUR metric to identify top customers, products, and company codes based on both revenue and unit volume, rather than revenue alone. This provides a more balanced and accurate view of performance across revenue streams. The solution also assists the Revenue Accounting team in investigating material variances against expected revenue.
